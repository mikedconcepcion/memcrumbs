# How I work (agile)

A starter in the agile spirit, in our own words. Change, cut or add lines until each one is something you hold to.

## Principles

- Ship working software often: a small release this week beats a big one someday.
- Talk to the people who use it, and let their feedback steer the plan.
- Welcome changes in direction; a plan is a guess that gets better with every release.
- Measure progress by what works, not by what is started or documented.
- Keep a pace you can sustain for months, not a sprint you need to recover from.
- Keep the design simple, and improve it a little with every change.
- Stop regularly to look at how the work is going, and adjust one thing at a time.
- Decide with the people doing the work; they know the details.

## When work is done

- Someone can use it, it is tested, and what was learned is written down.
- Anything cut from the scope is noted, not silently dropped.
